Why genuine

No fakes. Ever.

Fake body care is everywhere in Kenya — refilled bottles, copied labels, watered-down formulas. Here’s how we keep every item real.

Bought in US stores

Every product is bought at US retailers [STORE NAMES, e.g. Target, Walmart, Ulta — CONFIRM] and carried or shipped to Kenya by us. No middlemen, no market stalls.

Sealed and new

Items arrive in the original packaging with factory seals. Nothing is decanted, refilled or repackaged. The few duos sold without their outer box are marked on the product page.

Proof on request

Ask and we’ll share the US receipt for your item [CONFIRM], and every bottle keeps its batch code so you can check it with the brand.

How to spot a fake

Wherever you shop, check for these.

  • Blurry print, misspellings or a label that peels too easily
  • No batch code or expiry stamped on the bottle
  • A broken, loose or missing seal under the cap
  • A smell or texture that doesn’t match the original
  • Sizes the brand doesn’t make, like “1 L” of a brand that only sells 473 mL
  • A price far below what the item costs in the US
